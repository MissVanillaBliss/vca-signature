  1. Fill in your details, choose a banner and click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, click the Settings gear icon (top right).
  3. Go to Account (or Mail) → Signatures.
  4. Click + New signature and give it a name, e.g. "Viva con Agua".
  5. Click in the big signature box and paste with Ctrl + V.
  6. Under Choose default signatures, pick it for new messages and replies.
  7. Click Save.

  3. Click + to add a new signature and give it a name.
  4. Delete any placeholder text and paste with Cmd + V.
